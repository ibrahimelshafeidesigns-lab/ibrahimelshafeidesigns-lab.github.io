Studio

A small studio. A few homes at a time. Pieces made to be lived with, for a lifetime.

Al Shafei is Ibrahim Elshafei’s studio in Cairo. We sit with you and listen to how your home is lived in: who gathers around the table, where the morning coffee is made, what needs a quiet place of its own. We design around that, our own workshop builds it, and the same hands fit it in your home, so it stays calm and well made for years.
